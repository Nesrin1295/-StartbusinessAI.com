StartbusinessAI Prompt Pack: AI Content Writing

Use these prompts with ChatGPT, Claude, or another AI writing tool. Replace bracketed text with your niche, buyer, and offer.

1. Niche Positioning
Act as a content strategist. I want to offer AI-assisted content writing for [buyer type] in [niche]. Suggest 5 narrow service packages, the pain each solves, and a simple deliverable for each.

2. Client Research
Research the likely content problems for [buyer type]. List their goals, objections, audience, common offers, and topics they need to explain repeatedly.

3. Content Pillars
Create 5 content pillars for [buyer type] that help them attract [target customer]. For each pillar, give 10 post ideas and 3 blog ideas.

4. Blog Outline
Create an SEO-friendly outline for an article titled "[topic]" for [audience]. Include search intent, H2 sections, examples, and a clear call to action.

5. First Draft
Write a clear, useful first draft for this outline: [paste outline]. Use a helpful expert tone, avoid hype, and include practical examples.

6. Editing Pass
Edit this draft for clarity, structure, accuracy, and conversion. Keep the voice simple and professional: [paste draft].

7. Social Repurpose
Turn this article into 10 LinkedIn posts, 5 short captions, and 3 email newsletter ideas: [paste article].

8. Sample Pack
Create a sample content pack for [niche] that includes 3 posts, 1 blog intro, 1 email, and a short explanation of the strategy.

9. Monthly Retainer
Design a monthly content writing package for [buyer type]. Include deliverables, workflow, pricing tiers, revision rules, and client onboarding questions.

10. Outreach Message
Write 5 short outreach messages offering a content sample to [buyer type]. Make them specific, polite, and low pressure.

11. Content Calendar
Create a 30-day content calendar for [business] selling [offer]. Include post topic, format, hook, and CTA.

12. Quality Checklist
Create a checklist I can use before delivering AI-assisted content to a client.
