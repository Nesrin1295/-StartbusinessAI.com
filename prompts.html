Prompts that move your work forward

Use AI with better context, truthful constraints, and a clear goal. Replace anything in [brackets] before you send a prompt.

Get hired · ChatGPT / Claude

Tailor a resume truthfully

Find the strongest match without inventing experience.

Act as a careful resume editor. Compare my resume with the job description below. Rewrite my summary and up to 5 bullets to foreground relevant transferable skills and measurable evidence. Do not invent facts, titles, tools, or numbers. Mark missing information with [ADD EVIDENCE]. Resume: [paste]. Job description: [paste].
Customize: Add your target country, seniority, and preferred length. Remove personal contact details before pasting. Example outcome: clearer bullets aligned to the role's real priorities.
Interview · ChatGPT / Gemini

Build a strong STAR answer

Turn a genuine experience into a concise interview story.

Help me answer “[interview question]” using STAR. Ask me 4 short questions to uncover the real situation, task, action, and result. Then draft a 90-second answer in a confident, natural voice. Keep all facts truthful and flag where a specific result would strengthen it.
Tell the AI the role and company context. Practice aloud and rewrite any phrase you would never naturally say.
Freelancing · ChatGPT / Claude

Create a focused service offer

Connect one skill to one valuable client outcome.

I can [skills] and have experience with [industry or life experience]. Suggest 3 narrow freelance offers for [type of client]. For each, state the client problem, deliverable, realistic beginner scope, proof sample I can create, and a low-risk way to validate demand. Avoid income promises and generic services.
Specific skills and buyer types produce better ideas. Pick one offer and speak to potential clients before paying for tools.
Client outreach · ChatGPT

Write a respectful first message

Create personalized outreach without spammy claims.

Write a 90-word outreach message to [person/business]. Mention this specific observation: [real observation]. Explain how my [service] could help with [problem], include one relevant proof point [proof], and suggest a small next step. Be warm and direct. Do not use hype, fake urgency, or claim I reviewed anything I did not review.
Research each recipient and change the opening line. Quality matters more than sending many messages.
Career change · Claude / ChatGPT

Map transferable skills

Translate unpaid, caregiving, volunteer, and previous work fairly.

Help me identify transferable skills for a move from [past situation] to [target role]. Include paid work, caregiving, volunteering, study, and community responsibilities where relevant. For each skill, ask for a concrete example and suggest honest resume language. Separate demonstrated skills from skills I still need to build.
Give real examples, not only job titles. Never present unpaid experience as paid employment; label the context clearly.
Salary · ChatGPT / Claude

Prepare for a raise conversation

Build an evidence-based case and practice responses.

Help me prepare for a compensation conversation for [role/location]. Organize my evidence [paste achievements] into business impact, increased responsibility, and market context. Draft a concise opening request, 5 questions to ask, and calm responses to likely objections. Do not invent market data; identify what I should verify independently.
Research credible local salary data and decide your preferred result, minimum acceptable result, and non-salary alternatives.
Business · Perplexity / ChatGPT

Validate an idea before spending

Turn assumptions into small research tasks.

My idea is [idea] for [specific customer]. List the 7 riskiest assumptions about demand, access, pricing, delivery, and trust. For each, propose a free or low-cost test I can complete within 7 days, what evidence to collect, and what result would make me continue, change, or stop. Do not treat AI-generated opinions as customer evidence.
Use AI to design the test, then gather real evidence through conversations, searches, or small pre-sales.
Work · ChatGPT / Gemini

Rewrite a difficult email

Stay clear and professional while preserving your boundary.

Rewrite this email to be concise, warm, and firm. Preserve my decision and all factual details. Remove apologetic filler, blame, and ambiguous wording. Give me a direct version and a softer version, each under 140 words. Context: [context]. Draft: [paste without private information].
State the relationship and desired action. Check names, dates, commitments, and tone before sending.
Content · ChatGPT / Claude

Repurpose one useful idea

Create platform-specific drafts without losing substance.

Turn this original source [paste] into: one LinkedIn post, one 5-slide carousel outline, and one short email. Audience: [audience]. Goal: [goal]. Keep the same core insight, adapt structure to each format, and do not add statistics or claims not present in the source. End each with a practical next step.
Use your own source material and examples. Edit each draft so it sounds like you.
